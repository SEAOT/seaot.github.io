About

Krittapas (Bright) Chanchaiworawit leads the team’s two groups: SEA OtTeRS, which studies galaxies and active galactic nuclei across cosmic time using multi-epoch monitoring and deep surveys, and SEAL Lab, which develops single-point diamond-turned optics, precision assembly and laser metrology for astronomical instrumentation.
